About me

One pair of hands on the ad, the page and the tracking.

I’m a digital marketing strategist based in Electronic City, Bengaluru. I run an independent studio, Subramanyam Agency, across Chennai and Bengaluru for founder-led brands across India, and I intern at Leadnox on real estate lead generation.

What I’ve seen go wrong in marketing usually isn’t the ad. It’s the handoff: the creative made by one person, the page built by another, the tracking set up by a third, and nobody owning the gaps between them.

So I keep the whole loop in-house. I plan the offer, write, shoot and edit the creative, build the site it lands on, and read the result in Google Analytics 4, SQL and Power BI. When something breaks I can usually find it, because I built the thing it sits on.

I trained as an engineer and worked as a data analyst before I moved into marketing, which is why I tend to build my own tools instead of renting them. If an idea can’t survive on my own brand first, I don’t sell it.
